About the shop

I opened Ram Communication about three and a half years ago on Harsaon-Sadarpur Road. I started small, with a repair table and a few cables, because every neighbour had a phone that needed fixing and nowhere close to go.

People here call me the mobile surgeon. I took the name seriously: I open every phone carefully, show you the fault, and explain it in plain words. iPhones and smart watches are my special work, and I like the jobs other shops turn away.

Many of my customers now send their cousins and friends too. That is how a small shop grows, one fixed phone at a time.
